GPU PARAMS OPTIMISATION:
- Objective: find most efficient combination of gpu-params threads_per_block 
    and blocks while keeping the linear size = 64.

- Tried:
    homogeneous | threads | blocks | tiles | result | time (min) | mc attempts per s (mil) 
    --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    yes         | 1       | 16     | 4     | err1   | x          | x
    yes         | 2       | 8      | 4     | done   | 20         | 135
    yes         | 4       | 4      | 4     | warn1  | 42         | 115
    yes         | 8       | 2      | 4     | warn1  | 40         | 39
    yes         | 1       | 16     | 4     | err2   | x          | x
    -----

    err1 = CUDA_ERROR_COOPERATIVE_LAUNCH_TOO_LARGE
    err2 = CUDA_ERROR_INVALID_VALUE
    warn1 = NumbaPerformanceWarning

    Config. constrains:
    - cubic lattice: Lx = Ly = Lz;
    - tile linear size >= 4;
    - block size is multiple of 32;
    - at least 64 threads/block;
    - reasonable range is 128–256 threads/block;

    Legend:
    - Tb = tot. num of threads per block
    - T = tot. num. of threads
    - L = L_x, L_y, L_z = linear size
    - N = tot. num. of particles
    - tpb = threads_per_block
    - b = blocks
    
    NOTE: tiles fixed at (4, 4, 4)
